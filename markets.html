02 — DIFFERENT REQUIREMENTS

Different markets.
Different requirements.

The same package is rarely the right answer for every food application.

Temperature, shelf life, barrier performance, sealing, logistics, presentation and end-of-life requirements can change significantly from one market to another.

Our role is to understand those requirements and connect them with the right packaging technology.

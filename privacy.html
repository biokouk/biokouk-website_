Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how BIOKOUK handles personal information when you visit our website or contact us.

Last updated: October 2026
01

Who we are

BIOKOUK provides food packaging solutions and technical support to businesses in Greece.

For questions concerning this Privacy Policy or the handling of your personal data, you can contact us at:

biokouk@outlook.com

02

Information we may receive

You can browse the BIOKOUK website without directly providing personal information to us.

If you choose to contact us by email, we may receive information that you voluntarily provide, such as your name, email address, company, telephone number and the content of your message.

03

How we use your information

Personal information received through direct communication may be used to respond to enquiries, discuss packaging projects, provide requested information and manage professional or commercial communications.

We do not sell personal data.

04

Legal basis

Depending on the nature of the communication, personal data may be processed where necessary to respond to your request, take steps prior to entering into a business relationship, fulfil contractual or legal obligations, or pursue legitimate business interests in accordance with applicable data protection law.

05

Data retention

Personal information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, including the management of enquiries and business relationships, and where required to comply with applicable legal obligations.

06

Sharing of information

Personal information may be processed by service providers that support the operation of our communications or website infrastructure, where necessary and subject to applicable data protection requirements.

Information may also be disclosed where required by applicable law or a competent authority.

07

Cookies and website technologies

The current BIOKOUK website is designed as a primarily informational website.

If analytics, advertising, embedded third-party services or other technologies requiring consent are introduced in the future, this Privacy Policy and the website's cookie controls will be updated accordingly.

08

Your data protection rights

Subject to the conditions of applicable data protection law, you may have rights including access to your personal data, correction, deletion, restriction of processing, data portability and objection to certain processing.

Where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw that consent at any time without affecting the lawfulness of processing carried out before withdrawal.

To exercise your rights, contact: biokouk@outlook.com

09

Supervisory authority

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the competent data protection supervisory authority if you believe that the processing of your personal data infringes applicable data protection law.

10

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the website, our services or applicable requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page together with its revision date.

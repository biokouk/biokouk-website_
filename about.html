ABOUT BIOKOUK

Packaging expertise.
Built around your business.

BIOKOUK is a food packaging solutions partner focused on innovative, high-performance and sustainable packaging technologies.

We help food businesses in Greece identify, test and implement packaging solutions designed around their products, processes and operational needs.

01 — WHO WE ARE

More than
packaging.

Packaging decisions affect much more than the container itself.

They influence production efficiency, food protection, shelf life, logistics, presentation, sustainability and ultimately the customer experience.

That's why BIOKOUK approaches every project from the application first — understanding the product, process and requirements before recommending a packaging solution.

02 — WHAT WE DO

From packaging selection
to implementation.

We support food businesses throughout the packaging development process.

03

04

03 — OUR TECHNOLOGY NETWORK

European technology.
Local expertise.

We continuously explore innovative packaging technologies and materials from across Europe, selecting solutions that can deliver real value to food businesses in the Greek market.

Our focus is not on promoting a specific product. It is on understanding the application and identifying the packaging solution that best meets its technical, operational and sustainability requirements.

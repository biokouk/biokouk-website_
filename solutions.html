02 — APPLICATION FIRST

We don't start
with the package. We start with the food.

A packaging material can perform perfectly in one application and fail in another.

Product characteristics, temperature, shelf-life requirements, filling process, sealing equipment, logistics and end use all influence the final packaging decision.

That's why we evaluate the complete application before recommending a solution.
